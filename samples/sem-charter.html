1. Purpose & Background

This charter establishes scope, governance, and timeline for developing Roselawn University's five-year Strategic Enrollment Management (SEM) Plan, to be presented to the Board of Trustees for approval within the sponsor's first year in role.

2. Scope

  • Recruitment and admissions funnel analysis across new, transfer, and returning-adult populations.
  • Retention and persistence strategy, including early-alert workflows and financial-aid packaging review.
  • Housing and student-life capacity planning aligned to enrollment targets.
  • Financial aid leveraging strategy and net-tuition-revenue modeling.

5. Success Metrics

  • Board-approved five-year SEM Plan delivered within the sponsor's first year.
  • New student enrollment growth target established and tracked against Fall-to-Fall benchmarks.
  • Division-level KPI scorecards published and reviewed on a quarterly cadence.
