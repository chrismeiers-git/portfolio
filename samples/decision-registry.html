DR-044 · Phonetic Name / Preferred Pronunciation Storage

Approved
Roselawn University SIS Modernization Project  |  Decision Registry Briefing Doc  |  June 2026
Problem Statement
Commencement announcers, ID-card printing, and the LMS's name-pronunciation feature each need a reliable phonetic spelling or pronunciation guide for a student's name. No single field exists for it today.
Current State
Roselawn already collects this informally in at least three places: a commencement-only spreadsheet rebuilt every spring, a free-text note some advisors add to their own files, and a one-off field some faculty maintain in the LMS roster. None of the three sync with each other, and none are visible to Registrar or IT staff outside the office that built them.
Options Considered
OptionTrade-off
Add one Phonetic Name field to the core Person record, student-editable through self-serviceSingle source of truth feeding every downstream use; requires a schema change and a self-service form update
Leave it to each office to keep its own free-text noteNo development cost, but perpetuates three unsynced lists and commencement's annual rebuild
Adopt a third-party name-pronunciation add-on toolPurpose-built, but a new vendor and integration for a single field
Recommendation & Decision
Add a single Phonetic Name field to the core Person record, student-editable through self-service, surfaced to commencement staging, ID-card printing, and the LMS roster.
Rationale
One field maintained once, by the student, beats three unsynced lists maintained by three different offices, and removes commencement's annual rebuild entirely.
Owner / Status
Registrar & IR Lead  ·  Approved, scheduled for the next Person-record schema update

DR-047 · Department Naming Standards

Approved
Roselawn University SIS Modernization Project  |  Decision Registry Briefing Doc  |  July 2026
Problem Statement
The SIS caps department descriptions at a fixed character length, but Finance, HR, and the SIS carried inconsistent full names and abbreviations for the same departments, which created mismatches in cross-system reporting and broke automated integration mappings.
Current State
Legacy abbreviations were coined ad hoc over two decades, department by department, with no documented rubric. Reporting built on top of them has to reconcile spelling variants by hand before it can be trusted.
Options Considered
OptionTrade-off
Adopt one standardized abbreviation rubric (full name plus a short code) across all systemsRemoves ambiguity permanently; requires a one-time cleanup pass across existing department records
Leave legacy names as-is, reconciled case by case as mismatches surfaceNo upfront work, but the reconciliation burden never goes away
Rename departments only inside the new SIS, leave every other system on legacy namingFixes reporting inside the SIS but leaves HR and Finance out of sync with it
Recommendation & Decision
Adopt a single standardized department-naming rubric, full name plus a short abbreviated code, enforced across the SIS, HR, and Finance systems going forward.
Rationale
Removes a whole category of integration-mapping errors and reporting ambiguity, rather than reconciling mismatches indefinitely, one report at a time.
Owner / Status
IR / Data Lead  ·  Approved, rubric published and applied to new departments; legacy cleanup in progress

DR-048 · Student Forms Platform Recommendation

Open (pilot in progress)
Roselawn University SIS Modernization Project  |  Decision Registry Briefing Doc  |  August 2026
Problem Statement
Dozens of paper and PDF student forms are reproduced ad hoc across offices, each with its own routing and approval process and no shared platform or audit trail.
Current State
Most offices maintain their own fillable PDFs, routed by email with no consistent approval record. Forms are rebuilt from scratch when a policy changes, and there is no institution-wide inventory of which forms exist or who owns them.
Options Considered
OptionTrade-off
Standardize on the newly selected forms platform for all new and reengineered student forms, with automated approval routingOne platform for creation and routing, consistent with the vendor selection already underway; requires migrating existing forms over time
Continue with office-specific PDF forms and manual routingNo migration effort, but the audit-trail and consistency problems persist
Build custom forms individually in the university's low-code platform, office by officeFlexible, but duplicates effort the forms-platform vendor selection already covers
Recommendation & Decision
Standardize on the selected forms platform as the single home for all new and reengineered student forms, with automated routing for approvals.
Rationale
One platform for both creation and approval routing avoids duplicative tooling and keeps the vendor selection already underway from fragmenting further.
Owner / Status
Program Director  ·  Open, first three pilot forms in build
