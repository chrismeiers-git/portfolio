This playbook gives every Student Affairs functional area a single, common reference for recurring operational work: what happens, who owns it, and what "done" looks like. It replaces a set of scattered department procedures with one shared operating rhythm.

2. Recurring Operating Cadence

  • Weekly: functional-area leads submit a one-page status update (wins, risks, decisions needed) to the VP Student Affairs.
  • Monthly: cross-functional Student Affairs Council meets to review enrollment funnel metrics, retention flags, and open escalations.
  • Termly: each functional area publishes a scorecard against its annual KPI targets, reviewed with the President's Cabinet.
  • Annually: playbook itself is reviewed and reissued, incorporating the prior year's process changes.

3. New Student Crisis & Escalation Protocol

A condensed version of the standard protocol, intended as a quick-reference for frontline staff:

  • Step 1: frontline staff member documents the concern in the shared case-management system within one business day.
  • Step 2: Dean of Students triages severity and assigns a functional-area lead as case owner.
  • Step 3: case owner coordinates any needed referrals (counseling, conduct, financial aid, housing) and logs status weekly until closed.
  • Step 4: cases involving safety risk are escalated immediately to the VP Student Affairs and Campus Safety, outside the normal weekly cadence.
