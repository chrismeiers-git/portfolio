1. Purpose & Scope

This report documents the User Acceptance Testing (UAT) cycle for the Roselawn University SIS Modernization Program's migration from a legacy student system to a cloud-based Colleague-model SIS. It summarizes test coverage, pass rates, outstanding issues, and the sign-off pathway required before go-live authorization.

Scope covered three finance and integration areas: Chart of Accounts, GL Structure Validation, and Person Lookup. Testing ran across three cycles between February and April 2026, using business-owner-authored test scripts validated against the implementation partner's configuration.

5. Sign-Off Guide

  • Step 1, Business Owner Review: functional area lead executes assigned test scripts and records pass/fail results in the shared UAT tracker.
  • Step 2, UAT Lead Validation: UAT lead reviews results, confirms defect severity classification, and routes blocking issues to the implementation partner.
  • Step 3, Steering Committee Acceptance: functional areas with a pass rate at or above 95%, or with an approved conditional plan, are presented to the Steering Committee for formal acceptance.
  • Step 4, Go-Live Authorization: Program Director issues the go/no-go recommendation to the Executive Committee based on consolidated sign-off status.

6. Final Recommendation

Chart of Accounts and Person Lookup are fully signed off. GL Structure Validation is recommended for conditional go-live with the vendor patch and re-test scheduled prior to term start; a rollback contingency has been documented and accepted by the Steering Committee.
