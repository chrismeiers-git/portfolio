A one-page visual translation of the five-year Student Affairs strategic plan: the pillars, what each is meant to accomplish, and the metric leadership tracks it by. Meant to fit on a wall, not just in a binder.

Strategic Pillars

Access & Enrollment Growth

Grow new-student enrollment sustainably while holding the line on financial-aid discount rate. Tracked by: new-student headcount, discount rate.

Persistence & Completion

Close first-to-second-year retention gaps for first-generation and transfer students. Tracked by: fall-to-fall retention rate by student segment.

Student Experience & Belonging

Every student names one staff or faculty member who knows them by name. Tracked by: annual student-experience survey.

Operational Excellence

Every functional area operates from one shared playbook and a published KPI scorecard. Tracked by: scorecard publication rate, playbook currency.
