1. Purpose & Background

This charter establishes the governance structure and planning cadence for Roselawn University's institutional strategic plan refresh and the executive oversight structure supporting it, including alignment with any concurrent enterprise systems initiatives.

2. Scope

  • Development and Board approval of a five-year institutional strategic plan with measurable division-level KPIs.
  • Design and chartering of a standing Executive Steering Committee to maintain plan alignment across academic, enrollment, finance, and technology functions.
  • Integration of the strategic plan's data and reporting needs with the university's existing Power BI / SharePoint reporting infrastructure.

5. Success Metrics

  • Board approval achieved within the chartered nine-month timeline.
  • 100% of divisions operating from a published KPI dashboard within 60 days of plan approval.
  • Executive Steering Committee sustains monthly cadence with no more than one missed session per year.
