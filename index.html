Case Study · ERP & SIS Program Delivery

Ellucian Colleague SIS Modernization, $3.37M, Five Years

As PNWU's ERP Program Director / SIS Modernization Project Director, I'm the university's owner representative and project manager for a $3.37M, five-year (FY2025 to 2030) replacement of its student information system, migrating from PowerCampus to Ellucian Colleague SaaS across a three-party delivery model with Ellucian and Ferrilli. Second time I've held a client-side SIS mandate. The first was eight years as the University of Kansas Medical Center's co-executive sponsor for PeopleSoft SIS.

  • Negotiated a scope-reduction change order that moved 1,300 hours of integration work to the implementation partner, protecting the delivery timeline through an aggressive go-live schedule.
  • Caught an integration the original scope missed: a full Paylocity feed that creates the LDAP network account as staff are hired, replacing a manual process. Found before go-live, not after.
  • Co-chair the SIS Steering Committee and coordinate the SIS/ERP Executive Committee, running monthly governance across roughly 20 institutional systems and 20-plus stakeholders, with Ellucian Colleague and PeopleSoft tied together through Ethos integration middleware.
  • Ran a competitive vendor selection for a forms platform that integrates with every ERP PNWU runs, including Kuali Build, and am leading a similar selection now for a payment gateway provider.
  • Wrote the UAT methodology used across every functional area: validation plans, functional review guides, sign-off documentation, plus the field-level ownership and crosswalk sign-off behind the PowerCampus-to-Colleague conversion.

Case Study · AI-Built Project & Program Tooling

The Project Management Tooling I Built Inside the Tenant

Nobody sells a project management tool shaped like this program. Roughly 20 institutional systems, three organizations sharing the work, and a conversion running underneath all of it. So I built what we needed on the SharePoint, Power BI, and Power Platform licenses PNWU already owns, with Claude Enterprise, Cowork, and Copilot doing the generation and the cross-referencing. No new vendor review. No new license line. No data leaving the tenant.

  • UAT plan generation and tracking. A functional owner gets a validation plan with numbered steps, expected results, and a sign-off block instead of a blank document. 75 active plans and 381 UAT and data-testing steps since July 2025, tracked in SharePoint and rolled up in Power BI so the program can see what's actually been tested.
  • An Integration Master. Every integration in the program, its owner, its middleware path, and what it depends on, held in one place instead of in three organizations' spreadsheets.
  • An entry-grid generator behind the conversion work, including the 1,600-plus faculty and staff records moved off the legacy SIS.
  • A Decision Registry. Every governance call recorded with the question, the options considered, the decision, and who made it, so something settled in month four is still findable and attributable in month thirty.

None of it is bespoke to one institution. It's built on the stack most universities already run, and it keeps working when I'm not in the room.

Case Study · Governance, Sponsorship & Operational Depth

Executive Sponsorship, Board-Approved Plans & Enrollment Operations

Before I ran the implementation, I sponsored one, and before that I ran the offices that live inside the system. Eight years as co-executive sponsor for PeopleSoft SIS at KU Medical Center. Two decades as chief student affairs and enrollment officer at two institutions. It's why I read a functional requirement the way the people who have to live with it do.

  • At Kansas City Kansas Community College, led the cross-functional planning process that built and secured Board approval for a five-year Strategic Enrollment Management Plan within my first year, then worked with division leaders to stand up division-wide KPIs and an integrated strategic map. The division posted Fall 2023 results against it: headcount up 2.8%, FTE up 1.9%, new student enrollment up 8.5% year over year.
  • At WSU Tri-Cities, led the division that grew FTE enrollment 38% to an all-time high of 1,868 students over six years, chaired the student fee committee securing $400,000-plus annually for student programs, and worked with students and campus leadership to deliver a $7.5M Student Union Building through two referendums, construction, and operation. Chaired the IT Strategic Advisory Student Subcommittee while serving as campus CIO.
  • Tuition and fee governance ran throughout, including service on KU Medical Center's Tuition and Fees Policy Committee. Recognized nationally with NASPA Pillar of the Profession, American Council on Education Fellow, and NASPA Board of Directors, Region V.
