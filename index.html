Case Study · Systems Integration & Governance

Ellucian Colleague SIS Modernization

As PNWU's Interim ERP Program Director / SIS Modernization Project Director, I serve as the university's owner representative and project manager for a $3.37M, five-year (FY2025 to 2030) replacement of its student information system, migrating from PowerCampus to Ellucian Colleague SaaS across a three-party delivery model with Ellucian and Ferrilli. It's the second time I've held this kind of client-side SIS mandate: earlier in my career I was the University of Kansas Medical Center's co-executive sponsor for its student information systems (PeopleSoft SIS) for eight years.

  • Co-chair the SIS Steering Committee and coordinate the SIS/ERP Executive Committee, running monthly governance across roughly 20 institutional systems and 20-plus stakeholders.
  • Negotiated a scope-reduction change order that shifted 1,300 hours of integration work to the implementation partner, protecting the delivery timeline.
  • Guided PNWU through an aggressive go-live timeline while identifying a gap the original scope missed: a full Paylocity integration that automates LDAP network account creation as staff are hired, instead of a manual process.
  • Ran a competitive vendor selection for a forms platform to integrate with every ERP PNWU runs, including Kuali Build, and am now leading a similar selection for a new payment gateway provider.
  • Introduced a UAT methodology across every functional area (validation plans, functional review guides, sign-off documentation) and built the data governance framework behind the PowerCampus-to-Colleague migration.
  • System portfolio spans Ellucian Platforms (Colleague SIS) and PeopleSoft SIS, tied together through Ethos integration middleware, from a single-instance SIS up to a full enterprise ERP suite.

Case Study · Student Affairs & Enrollment Management

Chief Student Affairs & Enrollment Officer, Two Institutions

Before the SIS and ERP work, I spent two decades leading the student-facing side of higher education in partnership with admissions, enrollment, and student affairs teams: admissions, registrar, financial aid, housing, athletics, counseling, and career services, twice as chief student affairs and enrollment officer, across two different institution types: a public community college and a public regional four-year university.

  • At WSU Tri-Cities, a public regional four-year university, I led the division that grew FTE enrollment 38% to an all-time high of 1,868 students over six years, chaired the student fee committee (securing $400,000-plus annually for student programs), and worked with students and campus leadership to deliver a $7.5M Student Union Building through two student fee referendums, construction, and operation.
  • At KCKCC, a public community college, led the team that built and delivered a Board-approved five-year SEM Plan within my first year; the division then posted Fall 2023 results against it (headcount up 2.8%, FTE up 1.9%, and new student enrollment up 8.5% year over year), while directly overseeing Student Activities, a new Basic Needs Center, and the $20M, 254-bed residence hall's opening to full occupancy in year one.
  • Tuition and fee governance ran throughout this work, including service on KU Medical Center's Tuition and Fees Policy Committee; recognized nationally with NASPA Pillar of the Profession, American Council on Education Fellow, and NASPA Board of Directors, Region V.
